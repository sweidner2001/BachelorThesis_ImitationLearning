\chapter*{Directory of Aids}

The following digital \ac{AI} tools were used in the preparation of this thesis:

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{ChatGPT (OpenAI) \cite{ChatGPT}}

    Support for translation, brainstorming, rewriting of own ideas, searching for potential sources and image generation.

    \item \textbf{Perplexity \cite{Perplexity}}
    
    Support for translation, brainstorming, rewriting of own ideas and searching for potential sources.

    \item \textbf{DeepSeek \cite{DeepSeek}}

    Support for translation, brainstorming, rewriting of own ideas and searching for potential sources.

    \item \textbf{GitHub Copilot \cite{GitHubCopilotDein}}

    Support for translation, brainstorming, rewriting of own ideas, searching for potential sources and support with wording and structuring.

    \item \textbf{DeepL \cite{DeepLUbersetzerPraziseste}}

    Translation of text passages.

\end{itemize}
